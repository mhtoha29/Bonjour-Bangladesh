## FAQ `/faq/` — client-supplied question set, edited for clarity

H1: **Apparel sourcing questions, answered.**  
Lead: **Find practical answers about product development, partner facilities, quality, compliance and sending your first inquiry.**

1. **What is Bonjour Bangladesh?** A Dhaka-based garment buying house and apparel sourcing partner coordinating development, factory selection, production follow-up, quality and shipment readiness for international buyers.
2. **How long have you been operating?** Bonjour Bangladesh has served apparel buyers since 2010.
3. **Which markets do you work with?** We review inquiries from buyers in international markets and discuss the relevant product, quality, compliance and shipment requirements for each destination.
4. **What garments can you source?** Men’s, women’s and kids’ knit, woven and fully fashioned apparel, including suitable casual, fashion, activewear, denim and outerwear styles, depending on partner capability.
5. **Can we send a design or tech pack?** Yes. A tech pack, sketch, specification sheet, reference photo or product concept gives our team a starting point for development review.
6. **Can you arrange samples?** We can coordinate relevant development, fit, size-set, pre-production or photo samples according to the product and agreed scope.
7. **How is a factory selected?** We consider product expertise, capacity, technical capability, quality, compliance, cost and delivery requirements before recommending a partner.
8. **What is your MOQ?** There is no universal MOQ. It depends on the product, fabric, colour, construction and selected facility, so we assess it per inquiry.
9. **Can you source fabrics and accessories?** We can coordinate fabric, yarn, trim and accessory sourcing against the product specification and target requirements.
10. **How do you manage quality?** Relevant checkpoints can include pre-production review, material checks, inline inspection, measurements, workmanship, final inspection and packing checks.
11. **Do you conduct AQL inspections?** Where required, the agreed AQL level and inspection procedure are applied to the relevant order.
12. **What happens when a quality issue is found?** The issue is communicated to the manufacturing partner, corrective action is followed up and the affected work is re-checked where appropriate.
13. **Can you coordinate laboratory testing?** Yes, when required for a product or buyer programme. Tests are selected against the relevant specification and market requirements.
14. **Can you support compliance requirements?** We review buyer, product, facility and destination-market requirements and coordinate applicable follow-up with the partner facility.
15. **Can you explore preferred or recycled materials?** Yes, where suitable materials and supporting documentation are available for the product and buyer requirements.
16. **How is production followed up?** Our team communicates with the partner facility about progress, materials, approvals, workmanship, risks and shipment readiness.
17. **Can you meet our required shipment date?** We plan and follow up against an agreed schedule, while communicating risks and feasibility rather than making an unconditional guarantee.
18. **Do you coordinate shipping documents?** We can coordinate relevant packing, shipping and buyer-specific documents with the parties responsible for the order.
19. **Can you support a factory we already use?** This can be discussed for production follow-up, merchandising, QC or shipment coordination, depending on scope and access.
20. **What should we include in an inquiry?** Product type, image or tech pack, fabric, approximate quantity, colours, size range, destination, target date and quality/compliance requirements help us assess your brief. Send what you have.
21. **How do we request a quotation?** Use the product inquiry or Request a Quote form, or contact the team by email/WhatsApp. We review the requirements before discussing costing and next steps.
22. **How do we start working together?** Share a brief; we review the product, discuss sourcing and development, agree the scope, then coordinate the relevant stages from sampling to shipment readiness.

FAQ close: **Still have a specific requirement? Send your brief and our team can discuss the right starting point.**

## Editorial and legal publication gates

- **Insights:** no invented article titles, dates, named experts or case studies. The article template is ready; publish only original, expert-reviewed sourcing guidance with a real author and meaningful link to products/capabilities.
- **Factory Partner Gallery:** no public page until real photographs, facility permission and accurate captions exist. Never call partner factories “our factories”.
- **Hashmat Ali Foundation:** the client text is visible in a noindex staging page and homepage preview. Public indexing and any additional social-impact claims or imagery wait for identity, activities and rights approval.
- **Certification images:** the supplied folder provides only layout references for private staging. Public marks need current facility-level evidence and logo permission; the watermarked WRAP image and Bangladesh Bank/Government seals are not publishable certification badges.
- **Legal:** the supplied legal text is a draft. Privacy, Terms, Disclaimer and any Cookie Policy require review against the actual form data, analytics/AI vendors, attachment retention, hosting and applicable law before launch. Do not promise “we never disclose data” while third-party processors are in use.
- **Client review:** confirm UK/Europe experience, QC-team wording, MD bio/portrait, WhatsApp line, “25+” meaning and any partner/certification claims before public deployment. All other copy above deliberately avoids guarantees and vanity metrics.
