Do not convert the target markets United Kingdom, Germany, Spain, France, the Netherlands, Italy, United States and Canada into “countries we export to” without shipment evidence.

## Conditional claims

Use qualifiers such as “where required”, “depending on product and selected facility”, and “according to agreed buyer requirements” for:

- AQL inspections
- Laboratory testing
- Sustainable/recycled/organic materials
- Factory assessments and CAP coordination
- Compliance and test documentation
- Sample types
- Shipment documentation
- MOQ
- Lead time
- Target price and capacity

## Prohibited or hidden until verified

- Best, number one, leading or world-class
- Guaranteed quality or shipment date
- 100% compliant
- Owned-factory wording
- Buyer/client logos without permission
- Testimonials or case studies without written approval
- Factory count, monthly capacity, export volume or standard MOQ invented for visual balance
- Sustainability impact numbers without a measurement method
- Public certification logos without evidence and usage rights
- Watermarked assets

Unconfirmed numeric fields remain empty or display neutral copy such as “reviewed according to product and facility requirements”. They must never show fabricated demo values on production.

## Certification wording

Recommended section title:

> Standards and Audit Frameworks Available Through Selected Partner Facilities

Recommended disclaimer:

> Compliance and certification requirements are matched to the selected manufacturing facility, product and order. Availability and scope vary by facility and current certificate status. Supporting documents can be shared for qualified inquiries.

Admin requires:

- Partner facility/holder
- Scheme/framework
- Identifier
- Issuer or auditing body
- Scope
- Issue and expiry dates
- Verification URL
- Evidence file
- Logo permission
- Verified-by and verified-at

Specific rules:

- amfori BSCI and SMETA/Sedex are audit frameworks, not certifications
- ISO, GOTS, OEKO-TEX and WRAP need current facility evidence and mark permission
- Bangladesh Bank and Bangladesh Government seals are not certification badges
- The supplied WRAP image contains a watermark and cannot be published
- Supplied badges may appear only on private staging as pending-verification placeholders
